PaperPine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

PaperPine ("the app"), published by Sycamore Hill Studios, is a local-only app.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no analytics code and no advertising SDK.
  • Your documents stay on your device. Scanned pages and the PDFs you create are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and leave it only when you explicitly export or share them through Android's own file picker or share sheet.
  • Camera. The camera is used only to capture document pages, through Google's on-device document scanner (part of Google Play services). Images are processed on your device.
  • Text recognition. Text extraction (OCR) runs entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit. Your documents are not uploaded anywhere for processing. Play services may download the recognition model once; that download contains no data from you.
  • No accounts. The app requires no sign-up.
  • Purchases. Optional tips are processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The app never sees or stores your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting data. Deleting a document in the app removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

On iPhone

  • Same promise. PaperPine for iPhone collects nothing either: no account, no analytics, no ads and no network features. Your scans and PDFs stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Camera. Pages are scanned with iOS's built-in document camera and processed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Text recognition. Get text uses Apple's on-device text recognition; your pages never leave the phone.
  • Photos. Images you import are chosen through the iOS photo picker, so PaperPine has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Device backups. Like any app's data, it is included in your own iCloud Backup or computer backup if you have those turned on. Those backups belong to you and follow Apple's terms; we never see them.
  • Purchases. Optional supporter packs are one-time purchases processed entirely by Apple's App Store. The app never sees or stores your payment details; the App Store only tells it which pack your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock that pack's colors and art on your iPhone. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Ratings. If you're ever asked to rate the app, that request is shown by iOS itself.
  • Deleting data. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored there.
